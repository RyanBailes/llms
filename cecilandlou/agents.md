# Cecil & Lou

> Cecil & Lou is a family-owned children's clothing brand in Tyler, Texas, founded in 2011 by Ashley McCain and Blythe McCain. The brand sells classic Southern children's clothing for babies, girls, and boys, with a focus on smocking, embroidery, applique, monograms, heirloom styles, seasonal dressing, and coordinated sibling outfits.
